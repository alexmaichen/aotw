## Category of the Week : :four: FOWO :fist:
All four fists ran in a row. How much ME gambling is too much ME gambling? Up to you to decide.
Once you decide to no longer mercifully end your runs, run ZAP instead as discussed earlier.
Curse of Drowning can help with mid-game damage too, in the event you happen to come across it.

Here's the unmodded WR by [arcalena](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=07OvSmi5JxE) and the modded WR by [nalei](https://www.speedrun.com/hades/runs/yd49kgjy)!


## Category of the Week 272: :four: FOWO :fist:
If you have at least an hour to burn, consider this! Four fists, four runs, with Merciful End N times and ZAP 4-N times. Generally, the stronger fist aspects use ZAP and the weaker ones ME, but exactly how much ME gambling you want to do is up to you. There is also the midgame-option of Curse of Drowning on Talos specifically, though that won't help much in the lategame.

Here's the unmodded WR by [arcalena](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=07OvSmi5JxE) and the modded WR by [nalei](https://www.speedrun.com/hades/runs/yd49kgjy)!
